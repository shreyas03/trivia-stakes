THE GAME PLAN

Big bids. Bigger brains.

Start with 1,000 points. Play 8 rounds. Most points at the end wins. Tied leaders share the win.

  1. See the category. The question stays hidden while you bid.
  2. Bid for your turn. Raise your bid from 50 to 300 points, in steps of 10. All bids are visible. Highest goes first; tied bids go in the order they were placed.
  3. Take your shot. Choose from four options and lock your answer in within 15 seconds. Only the answering player sees the options. Correct? Earn your bid plus the pool. Wrong or too slow? Lose your bid into the pool; the next bidder tries.
QUICK EXAMPLE

You have 1,000 points and bid 70. Another player misses and adds 100 to the pool. Your correct answer earns 170. Your total: 1,170.

What if everyone misses?

A bonus buzzer sequence starts, with its own explanation before the question. No new wagers. First to buzz answers; correct wins the pool. A miss removes that player from the whole bonus sequence, and the others get a fresh question. If everyone misses, or nobody buzzes within 20 seconds, the pool clears.

You only lose a bid if you get an answer attempt. You can pass by not bidding. You cannot wager more points than you have. Everyone, including players who passed, can play the bonus. Buzzer order is decided by arrival at the server.
